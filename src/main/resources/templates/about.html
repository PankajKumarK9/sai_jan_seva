Our Story

Sai Jan Kalyan Seva Sansthan was founded by Roop Kishor with a simple vision: make quality computer education accessible to every student in Kasganj and nearby areas.

हमारा लक्ष्य है कि हर युवा को कंप्यूटर की बेसिक से एडवांस तक की शिक्षा मिले, जिससे वे अपने करियर में आगे बढ़ सकें।

  • Hindi and English medium classes
  • Experienced trainers
  • Recognized certificates
  • Job placement assistance
  • Affordable fee structure

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable computer education to every student and youth in our community.

Our Vision
